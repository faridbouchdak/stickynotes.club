# Use sticky notes for yourself

Keep the things you want to remember on your own private board. You can start free with Club Member; you do not need to run workshops or publish anything.

## Before you start

You need a StickyNotes.club account. [Create a free account](https://stickynotes.club/register), then log in. You can work on your own board before confirming your email address.

Club Member includes one active, editable private board. Notes on the board do not use the daily allowance for publishing on the worldwide wall.

## Make a week planner

1. Open **My boards** and select **New board**.
2. Name the board **My week**.
3. Choose **Week planner**, then create the board. Leave workshop mode off.
4. Add three sticky notes with things you want to do this week, such as *Prepare Tuesday's meeting*, *Book the bike repair* and *Read the project proposal*.
5. Move each note to the weekday when you want to work on it.
6. Come back tomorrow and move or edit your own notes as your plans change.

You now have a place to return to, with your own next steps visible. The examples are ordinary notes; the product does not book appointments or complete tasks for you.

## Prefer an ideas board?

Choose **Blank** when creating the board and name it **Ideas to explore**. Add one idea per note and arrange the notes as you think. You can change an existing board's layout later; you do not need a second board just to reorganise it.

On the free plan, use your existing board for your next experiment. Creating another owned board may require a paid plan. Do not delete a useful board just to try a different layout: whole-board deletion is permanent.

## Who can see it?

An unshared private board is hidden from other ordinary users and does not appear on the worldwide wall. Authorised platform staff may access private content when moderation, support, security or legal work requires it. See [privacy and safety](/privacy-and-safety/).

You do not have to create a Public sticky note first. Public notes and private-board notes are separate; there is no built-in move or copy between them.

## When would I upgrade?

Choose **Club Host** if you need more owned boards or want to invite others to your boards. Choose **Club Facilitator** if you want to run guided workshops. Continue using Club Member if one personal board is enough. [Compare plans and current prices](/plans-and-subscriptions/).
