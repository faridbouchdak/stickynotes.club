# StickyNotes.club social warm-up campaign

Start date: Saturday 29 August 2026  
Campaign window: 29 August-27 September 2026  
Primary goal: qualified visits to https://stickynotes.club/

This package contains the marketing plan, a 30-day content calendar, a shared image system and a separate folder for every requested social channel.

## Language allocation

- LinkedIn: Dutch and British English as separate posts.
- Facebook and WhatsApp Status: Dutch.
- X, Instagram, Reddit, Bluesky, Mastodon and WIP.co: British English.

## Important publishing rule

The account warm-up comes before repeated promotion. The opening post introduces the problem and the maker; product links are soft or placed in a first comment. Reddit is warmed through useful comments before any post, and WIP.co only receives updates about work that was actually completed.

## Facts

- StickyNotes.club publicly supports guided workshops, participation by link or QR code without an account or installation, silent brainstorming, shared timers, hidden voting results, a finished results view, PDF printing and Markdown copying.
- Workshop mode is available on Club Facilitator and Chosen Few.
- A workshop board supports up to 50 participants.
- The public homepage and relevant Help Centre pages returned successful responses when checked on 27 August 2026.
- The website records UTM parameters as of 30 August 2026. Verified in the codebase: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` and `utm_content` are stored against the `homepage_viewed` event and shown under "Campagnebronnen" on the admin dashboard.
- Attribution stops at the landing page. A tagged visit is counted; whether that same visitor later registers is not tracked. Carrying the source through the funnel is a separate decision, deliberately deferred because it would tie a campaign source to the moment an account is created.
- The measurement is server-side and stores no personal data: no user id, no IP address, no user agent, no session key. It therefore counts events, not unique visitors, and a visitor who opens the same link twice counts twice.
- UTM values are normalised to lowercase and limited to letters, digits, `.`, `-` and `_`, at most 32 characters. All URLs in section 7 of the marketing plan pass unchanged.

## Assumptions

- All named accounts are personal profiles and can publish publicly.
- No testimonials, customer logos or performance figures are approved for publication.

## Recommendations

- Use real screenshots whenever a post makes a product claim. Never ask image generation to invent product UI.
- Use the editorial visual only for problem-led or founder-led posts.
- Reply to every substantive comment within 24 hours.
- Do not post the same copy unchanged across channels.
- Do not use engagement pods, bulk DMs or automated Reddit promotion.

## Open questions for the review after 30 days

- Which audience segment produces the most qualified website visits?
- Which profile descriptions and pinned posts need updating?
- Is a free trial, pilot or demo the best conversion step after a website visit?
- Which screenshots can become durable campaign assets?
