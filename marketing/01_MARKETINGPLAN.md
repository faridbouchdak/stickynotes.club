# Marketing plan: warm up first, then prove the product

## 1. Objective

Generate qualified visits to https://stickynotes.club/ from independent facilitators, trainers, agile coaches, HR/L&D professionals and consultants.

The first 30 days are not a launch blast. They rebuild familiarity and trust on quiet personal profiles, then introduce the product through useful workshop knowledge and verified product proof.

## 2. Positioning

### Canonical promise

**A place where ideas can grow together.**

### Campaign lead

**Run a focused workshop in minutes.**

### Practical marketing position

StickyNotes.club is a deliberately focused workshop environment: enough control to guide a group from individual input to a shared result, without presenting itself as an infinite canvas, project suite or substitute facilitator.

### What we may truthfully say

- People can join a workshop through a link or QR code without an account or installation.
- A facilitator can use a live instruction and a shared timer.
- Silent brainstorm hides other people's notes until the facilitator reveals all notes.
- Voting results remain hidden while a voting round is open.
- A finished workshop produces a read-only result that can be printed or saved as PDF and copied as Markdown.
- Workshop mode is included with Club Facilitator and Chosen Few and supports up to 50 participants.

### What we do not claim

- That software replaces facilitation, judgement, psychological safety or decision-making.
- That changes are instant; current product language says they usually appear within a few seconds.
- That the product has an infinite canvas, breakouts, video, chat, a large integration catalogue, framework templates or cross-session analytics.
- That a private board makes copying impossible.
- That using the product guarantees time savings or better decisions.

## 3. Audience jobs and messages

| Audience | Job they are trying to do | Opening message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent facilitator | Guide the room and preserve a usable result | Spend less attention managing the mechanics of the board |
| Trainer | Let every participant contribute without a long technical start | Let people join from a link or QR code |
| Agile coach | Run a focused retro from silent input through prioritisation | Keep the flow deliberate without a sprawling canvas |
| HR/L&D | Gather perspectives with a low participant threshold | Make contributing understandable without an install |
| Consultant | Turn workshop input into a result worth sharing | Finish with decisions, actions, questions and a structured result |

These are message angles, not performance promises.

## 4. Content pillars

1. **The craft of facilitation - 40%**  
   Questions, writing rules, silent input, grouping, voting and turning ideas into decisions.

2. **The hidden work after a workshop - 20%**  
   Preserving raw input, separating ideas from decisions and reducing avoidable transcription.

3. **Building StickyNotes.club - 20%**  
   Product decisions, deliberate constraints and completed work. Especially useful on WIP.co.

4. **Verified product proof - 20%**  
   Real screenshots or short recordings of joining, silent brainstorm, timer, voting and results.

## 5. Warm-up sequence

### Days 1-7: re-enter the conversation

- Introduce the problem being explored.
- Ask concrete questions that practitioners can answer from experience.
- Respond thoughtfully; do not turn replies into sales conversations.
- Mention StickyNotes.club, but keep the website link soft.
- On Reddit, comment only.

### Days 8-14: teach before demonstrating

- Share facilitation practices that work with or without the product.
- Publish the first direct website link.
- Introduce one product behaviour at a time.
- Make the first Reddit post only after checking the target community's current rules.

### Days 15-21: prove the workshop flow

- Show joining, silent contribution, reveal, discussion, voting and the result.
- Use real product media.
- Pair each feature with the user outcome and a material limitation where needed.

### Days 22-30: invite evaluation

- Explain who the product is and is not for.
- Share a workshop checklist.
- Invite a visit to the site without artificial urgency.
- Review results and keep only sustainable channels and formats.

## 8. Metrics

### Essential now

- Homepage visits per UTM source, read from the admin dashboard. These are page views and not unique visitors or sessions; compare them against the untagged total shown beside them, because a campaign number means nothing without the ordinary traffic next to it.
- Substantive replies from people in the target audiences.
- Profile visits after posts.
- Link clicks where the channel exposes them.
- Saves or bookmarks on educational posts.

### Diagnostic, not a goal by itself

- Impressions.
- Likes.
- Follower growth.

### 30-day decision rule

Keep a channel in the active mix if it produces at least one of:

- qualified website visits;
- useful practitioner conversations;
- repeat engagement from the target audience; or
- product insight that changes copy, onboarding or the roadmap.

## 9. Weekly operating rhythm

- Monday: draft and verify claims for the week.
- Tuesday: publish a problem-led or educational post.
- Wednesday: reply, comment and collect audience language.
- Thursday: publish proof or a strong point of view.
- Friday: prepare media and alt text.
- Saturday: publish a founder/build post.
- Sunday: no required post; review and reply.

## 10. Publication gate

Before every post:

- Is the stated behaviour live and supported by the Help Centre?
- Is the outcome clear before the feature?
- Is any important access, plan, privacy or permanence limitation omitted?
- Does the copy remain understandable without the image?
- Is the image real when it depicts product UI?
- Is the alt text useful?
- Does the CTA lead directly to what the post promises?
- For Reddit: were the current subreddit rules checked immediately before posting?
- For WIP.co: was the stated work actually completed?
