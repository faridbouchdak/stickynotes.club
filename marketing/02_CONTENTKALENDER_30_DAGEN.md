## LinkedIn language rhythm

- Do not put full Dutch and English duplicates in one post.
- Publish the Dutch opening on Saturday 29 August and the English version on Monday 31 August.
- Thereafter alternate languages. When a topic deserves both versions, separate them by at least 48 hours and change the opening example.

## Minimum daily engagement routine

1. Reply to existing comments.
2. Leave two substantive comments on posts by people in the target audiences.
3. Save one phrase or question that could improve the next post.
4. Stop after 15 minutes unless a real conversation is underway.
