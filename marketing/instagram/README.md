Quiet account. Busy workshop table.

I have been building around a question that many facilitators know well:

**How can a group think together without the tool taking over the session?**

Software cannot create a clear question, psychological safety or a sound decision. Those remain part of the facilitator’s craft.

It can, however, remove some of the practical friction around collecting ideas, keeping the room focused and preserving the result.

That is the thinking behind StickyNotes.club.

Over the next few weeks I will share workshop practices, product decisions and what I am learning as I build.
