## First post - British English

I have been quiet here for a while.

Not because little was happening. I kept returning to a question that many facilitators will recognise:

**How can a group think together without the facilitator spending half the session managing the board?**

A tool cannot fix an unclear question, group dynamics or weak decision-making. It can remove some practical friction around joining, collecting ideas, keeping the work organised and preserving the result.

That question sits behind StickyNotes.club, the focused workshop environment I am building around sticky notes.

Over the next few weeks, I will share what I am learning about silent brainstorming, grouping, voting, decisions and the work that often remains after everyone leaves.

To start:

**Which part of a workshop creates the most work for you afterwards?**
