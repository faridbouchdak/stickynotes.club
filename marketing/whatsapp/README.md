## Verified production specification

The four definitive Post 01 assets were checked as:

- 1080 x 1920 px.
- 9:16 portrait.
- sRGB JPEG.
- Four separate, numbered files.
- Large dark text on a soft light panel.
- Important wording is present as text and does not depend on colour alone.

For future Status posts:

- Keep all essential words between y=250 and y=1650 px.
- Use at least 64 px body text where the amount of copy permits it.
- Maintain strong dark-on-light contrast.
- Do not rely on generated lettering; add final wording after image generation.
- Do not invent product interfaces. Use verified screenshots when making product claims.

## Image direction

Use a calm, credible editorial workshop style with restrained StickyNotes.club colours:

- purple `#E0B0FF`;
- pink `#FF7EB9`;
- yellow `#FFF740`;
- mint `#98FB98`;
- ink `#1F2937`;
- soft background `#FFF9FC`.

For WhatsApp Status, compose vertically at 9:16. Keep the central text area calm and low-detail, place people and workshop materials mainly in the lower half, and leave the top and bottom clear for interface overlays. Avoid generated writing, logos, QR codes, readable software interfaces, staged stock-photo poses and visual clutter.

## Accessibility

WhatsApp Status does not provide the same durable alt-text workflow as some feed platforms. Keep every frame concise, large and high-contrast, and make sure the wording remains understandable without interpreting the image.
